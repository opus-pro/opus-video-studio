## Transfer rule

Keep the optical material, decisive spatial travel, restrained type, foreground/background relationships and object continuity. Replace the objects and actions according to the new product. Citation-to-brand suits a research product; a generation product may carry an input into an output instead. Do not force research documents or citations into unrelated products merely because they are in this sample.
